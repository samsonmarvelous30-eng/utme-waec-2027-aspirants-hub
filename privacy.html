Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UTME & WAEC 2027 Aspirants Hub ("we", "our" or "the website") handles information when you visit and use our educational practice platform.

1. Information We Handle

Our website provides practice questions and quizzes for students preparing for examinations. Your quiz answers and results may be processed within your browser to display your performance.

Depending on the website features you use, your browser, hosting provider or other services may process technical information such as browser type, device information and website access logs.

If you email us, we will receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your enquiry.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the website.
  • Display quiz results and practice feedback.
  • Respond to messages and enquiries.
  • Understand technical problems and improve the website.
  • Support advertising services if they are introduced.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We may introduce advertising services, including Google AdSense. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise advertisements, subject to applicable settings and policies.

Advertising and analytics services, if used, may process information according to their own privacy policies. We will update this policy if our use of these services changes.

You can manage available cookie and advertising preferences through your browser or the relevant service settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information when visitors access the site. If we add other services, such as analytics or advertising, those providers may also process information under their respective policies.

5. Data Security

We aim to take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no website or method of internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for students preparing for examinations, including younger students. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the website or by email. Parents and guardians can help younger users understand safe internet practices.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you. You may contact us by email to make a privacy-related enquiry.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website or its services change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
